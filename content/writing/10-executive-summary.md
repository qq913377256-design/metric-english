# Writing an Executive Summary

## Workplace Context

虚构B2B平台 Atlas Cloud 的管理层将在15分钟会议中决定下季度资源优先级。你的分析包含增长、留存、产品稳定性和成本，摘要需要让管理者快速看出最重要的结果和选择。

## Data Brief

- 季度收入增长8%，目标为10%
- 新客户收入增长16%，扩展收入下降4%
- 企业客户首次续费率从78%降至71%
- 自动化功能采用率高的企业客户续费率高15个百分点
- 数据导入失败率从1.4%升至3.8%
- 新增两名实施顾问的季度成本为7.2万美元
- 管理层要决定实施支持和数据导入修复的优先级

## Model Email

**Subject: Q3 summary — acquisition growth, retention and reliability need action**

Hi leadership team,

Q3 revenue grew 8%, below the 10% target. New-customer revenue increased 16%, but expansion revenue fell 4%, and first-renewal rate for enterprise accounts declined from 78% to 71%.

Two issues require action. Enterprise customers with high automation adoption renew 15 points more often, while data import failures rose from 1.4% to 3.8%. I recommend prioritising the import fix and testing earlier automation setup with new enterprise accounts.

The proposed two implementation consultants would cost $72,000 per quarter; the pilot should measure renewal impact before permanent hiring.

Best,  
Lin

## Structure Breakdown

1. 第一段只保留目标差距和增长质量
2. 第二段把多个发现收敛为两个优先问题
3. 建议对应问题，并说明先修复还是先试点
4. 给出成本、验证条件或决策边界

## Language Toolkit

- **grew X, below the Y target**：同时交代结果和目标
- **Two issues require action.**：明确管理层需要关注的数量
- **I recommend prioritising X and testing Y.**：区分确定动作和待验证动作
- **before permanent hiring**：给资源承诺设置验证条件

## Model Task

写60–90词管理摘要：

- 月收入增长5%，目标7%
- 新客户增长12%，老客户收入下降3%
- 退款率从2%升至3.1%
- 建议先检查老客户流失和退款原因
- 周五前需要决定是否增加留存预算

## Model Reference

Monthly revenue grew 5%, below the 7% target. New-customer revenue increased 12%, but existing-customer revenue fell 3%, showing that growth is not yet supported by retention.

Refund rate also increased from 2.0% to 3.1%. Before Friday's budget decision, I recommend identifying the customer groups driving the revenue decline and reviewing the main refund reasons. Retention spend should be targeted only after those drivers are clear.

## Guided Task

下面的摘要没有优先级，也没有连接决策：

> Revenue grew. New customers did well. Expansion was down. Renewal was down. Automation looks good. Imports got worse. We may need consultants.

写90–130词，使用Data Brief中的关键数字，把问题收敛成两个优先事项，并说明7.2万美元顾问成本应怎样验证。

## Guided Reference

**Subject: Q3 priorities for enterprise retention**

Hi leadership team,

Revenue increased 8% in Q3, missing the 10% target. Acquisition remained strong, with new-customer revenue up 16%, but expansion revenue fell 4%. Enterprise first-renewal rate also declined from 78% to 71%.

The two immediate priorities are product reliability and early adoption. Import failures rose from 1.4% to 3.8%, while customers with high automation adoption renew 15 percentage points more often.

I recommend fixing the import issue and running an automation setup pilot before committing to permanent staffing. Two additional consultants would cost $72,000 per quarter, so the pilot should show measurable renewal improvement before hiring.

Best,  
Lin

## Independent Task

虚构数据产品 SignalDesk 的季度情况：

- 收入增长11%，超过8%目标
- 增长主要来自年付升级，新增客户数只增长2%
- 中型客户90天留存从76%降至69%
- 报表加载时间从2.2秒升至3.6秒
- 使用新版模板的客户每周活跃率高12个百分点，但不能确认因果
- 可选动作：性能修复需要6个工程周；模板引导试点需要2名顾问、为期6周

写120–160词管理摘要，给出结果质量、两个风险、资源优先级和验证方式。

## Independent Reference

**Subject: Q4 priorities — protect retention while sustaining upgrade growth**

Hi leadership team,

Quarterly revenue grew 11%, above the 8% target, but most of the increase came from annual-plan upgrades. New-customer volume rose only 2%, and 90-day retention for mid-market accounts fell from 76% to 69%. The headline growth therefore does not remove the need to address customer health.

Two issues deserve priority. Report load time increased from 2.2 to 3.6 seconds, creating a clear product reliability risk. Customers using the new templates are also 12 percentage points more active each week, although this relationship does not confirm causation.

I recommend allocating the six engineering weeks to performance recovery first and running the six-week template guidance pilot in parallel with two consultants. Success should be measured through load time, template adoption, weekly activity, and mid-market retention before either programme expands.

Best,  
Lin

## Final Task

这是与第1天结构相当、数据不同的15分钟终测。写120–160词邮件：

虚构公司 BrightCart 本月出现以下变化：

- 购买转化率从5.2%升至5.8%
- 增长集中在桌面端，移动端保持4.6%
- 平均订单金额下降7%
- 新折扣在桌面端默认展示
- 净收入只增长1.5%
- 团队尚未确认折扣是否带来有利润的增量

写给产品经理，说明发生了什么、证据支持什么、还不能确定什么，以及你建议的下一步。

## Final Reference

**Subject: Purchase conversion gain needs a margin check**

Hi Jordan,

Purchase conversion increased from 5.2% to 5.8% this month, but the gain is concentrated on desktop. Mobile conversion remained at 4.6%. At the same time, average order value fell 7%, leaving net revenue only 1.5% higher.

The timing is consistent with the new desktop discount increasing completed purchases, but the current result does not show whether those orders produced profitable incremental revenue. I recommend comparing contribution margin and repeat purchase by discount exposure, with mobile users as an additional benchmark.

I will share the margin and customer-level results before Thursday's promotion review. Until then, I would keep the discount at its current exposure rather than expand it.

Best,  
Lin

## Oral Retell

用1分钟向管理层说出一个核心结果、两个风险、资源先后顺序和判断成功的指标。
