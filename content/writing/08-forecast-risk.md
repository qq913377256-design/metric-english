# Explaining Forecast Risk

## Workplace Context

虚构家居品牌 Cedar & Co. 正在制定第四季度库存计划。财务负责人希望得到一个明确销售数字，但促销强度和供应到货时间仍不确定。你需要把预测写成可用于决策的范围。

## Data Brief

- 基准预测：第四季度收入1,840万美元
- 合理区间：1,680万至2,010万美元
- 基准假设：促销折扣与去年相同，缺货率低于4%
- 若两批进口商品延迟，收入可能减少90万美元
- 若促销点击率提高15%，收入可能增加70万美元
- 库存决策需要在周五前完成

## Model Email

**Subject: Q4 revenue forecast range and inventory risk**

Hi Finance team,

The baseline Q4 revenue forecast is $18.4 million, with a reasonable range of $16.8 million to $20.1 million. The baseline assumes last year's discount level and a stockout rate below 4%.

The largest downside risk is a delay to two imported product batches, which could reduce revenue by about $0.9 million. A 15% improvement in campaign click-through rate would add roughly $0.7 million in the upside case.

For Friday's inventory decision, I recommend planning to the baseline while protecting supply for the highest-margin products.

Best,  
Lin

## Structure Breakdown

1. 先给基准预测和合理区间
2. 明确基准依赖的关键假设
3. 量化一个主要下行情景和上行情景
4. 将范围转化为当前决策建议

## Language Toolkit

- **with a reasonable range of...**：把预测写成范围
- **The baseline assumes...**：暴露关键假设
- **could reduce revenue by about...**：量化下行风险
- **plan to the baseline while protecting...**：给出兼顾风险的行动

## Model Task

写60–90词预测说明：

- 下月订单基准为52,000
- 区间47,000至58,000
- 假设网站流量稳定、转化率不低于4.2%
- 大客户促销若延期，减少4,000单
- 建议排班按52,000单，保留临时运力

## Model Reference

Next month's baseline forecast is 52,000 orders, with a reasonable range of 47,000 to 58,000. The baseline assumes stable website traffic and conversion of at least 4.2%.

If the major-customer campaign is delayed, volume could fall by about 4,000 orders. I recommend scheduling core staff for the 52,000-order case while keeping temporary delivery capacity available for the upper range.

## Guided Task

把单点承诺改成90–130词风险说明：

> Q4 revenue will be $18.4 million. We are confident and should buy inventory for that number.

加入预测区间、两个基准假设、一个上下行情景，并给出周五库存决策建议。

## Guided Reference

**Subject: Planning range for the Q4 forecast**

Hi Finance team,

Our baseline Q4 revenue forecast is $18.4 million, but the planning range is $16.8 million to $20.1 million. The midpoint assumes that promotional discounts remain close to last year and that stockouts stay below 4%.

The main downside is a possible delay to two imported product batches, which could lower revenue by around $0.9 million. If campaign click-through improves 15%, the upside case adds about $0.7 million.

For Friday's inventory decision, I recommend using the baseline for core purchases and limiting additional commitments to products that sell well across all three scenarios.

Best,  
Lin

## Independent Task

虚构旅游平台 Wayfarer 的暑期客服需求预测：

- 基准为每周31,000个咨询，区间27,000至37,000
- 假设航班取消率为去年的3.5%
- 取消率每增加1个百分点，约增加3,800个咨询
- 新自助退款功能可能减少2,500个咨询
- 人力计划下周一确定，临时员工需要两周培训

写120–160词说明预测范围、关键假设、上下行情景和人力建议。

## Independent Reference

**Subject: Summer support demand range and staffing plan**

Hi Operations team,

The baseline forecast is 31,000 support contacts per week, with a planning range of 27,000 to 37,000. The baseline assumes an airline cancellation rate close to last year's 3.5%.

Cancellation rate is the largest upside risk to workload. Each additional percentage point would create approximately 3,800 contacts per week. In the lower-demand case, the new self-service refund flow could remove about 2,500 contacts, although its adoption rate is not yet known.

Because temporary agents require two weeks of training, I recommend staffing the core team for the 31,000-contact case and pre-approving a temporary group that can be activated if cancellations move above 4%. We should review the forecast weekly as airline schedules change.

Best,  
Lin
