# Clarifying a Vague Data Request

## Workplace Context

你在虚构电商公司 Northstar Market 担任数据分析师。区域销售经理发来一句话：Can you look at our sales performance? 她希望周五会议使用结果，但没有说明地区、时间范围或要支持的决策。

## Data Brief

- 最近一次正式销售复盘覆盖今年第一季度
- 公司有 East、Central、West 三个区域
- 销售经理周五要决定第二季度促销预算
- 你可以在周三下班前完成一版分析

## Model Email

**Subject: Scope for the Q1 regional sales review**

Hi Maya,

I can prepare the sales review by Wednesday. To make sure it supports Friday's budget discussion, could you confirm whether the main decision is how to allocate Q2 promotional spend?

I suggest comparing Q1 revenue and order volume across the East, Central, and West regions, with last quarter as the baseline. I can also highlight the product categories driving the largest changes. Please let me know if you need customer segment results as well.

Best,  
Lin

## Structure Breakdown

1. **Confirm delivery**：先说明能否完成以及交付时间
2. **Name the decision**：确认分析要支持的业务决策
3. **Propose a scope**：主动给出时间、地区、指标和比较基准
4. **Ask one focused question**：把需要对方决定的内容压缩成一个问题

## Language Toolkit

- **To make sure it supports...**：说明你为什么需要澄清
- **Could you confirm whether...**：礼貌确认一个关键选择
- **I suggest comparing A across B, with C as the baseline.**：提出可执行的分析范围
- **Please let me know if you need... as well.**：给出一个可选扩展

## Model Task

使用新信息改写一封60–90词的澄清邮件：

- 需求：Please analyse customer performance.
- 决策：客户成功团队要决定先联系哪些客户
- 建议范围：过去90天、续约时间、产品使用率、客服工单
- 交付：周四中午

邮件必须包含建议范围、业务决策和一个确认问题。

## Model Reference

**Subject: Scope for the customer performance review**

Hi Elena,

I can share an initial review by Thursday noon. To support the outreach plan, could you confirm whether the team wants to prioritise customers with the highest renewal risk?

I suggest reviewing accounts that renew within the next 90 days and comparing product usage, support tickets, and recent engagement. I can rank the accounts by risk and highlight the main reason behind each score.

Best,  
Lin

## Guided Task

下面的邮件范围过宽，也没有说明分析服务于什么决策：

> Hi Maya, I will look at all the sales data and send some charts. Do you need anything else?

把它改写成90–130词。必须写清周三交付、周五预算决策、三个区域的第一季度收入和订单量、以上季度为基准，并询问是否按产品类别拆分。

## Guided Reference

**Subject: Proposed scope for the Q1 sales analysis**

Hi Maya,

I can send the analysis by Wednesday so that you have time to review it before Friday's Q2 budget meeting.

To keep the work focused on the promotion decision, I propose comparing Q1 revenue and order volume across the East, Central, and West regions. I will use last quarter as the baseline and flag any region with a material change.

Could you confirm whether you also want the results split by product category? If so, I will include the top three categories driving each regional change.

Best,  
Lin

## Independent Task

运营负责人提出：Can you check delivery performance before Monday?

- 她要决定是否给 Harbor 和 Ridge 两个仓库增加周末班次
- 可比较最近8周的准时送达率、平均延迟小时数和订单量
- 你能在周五下午交付
- 还不清楚是否需要按承运商拆分

写一封120–160词邮件，确认决策、提出分析范围、说明交付时间并提出一个关键问题。

## Independent Reference

**Subject: Scope for the warehouse delivery review**

Hi Priya,

I can send the delivery review by Friday afternoon, which should leave time for questions before Monday's staffing decision.

To assess whether Harbor and Ridge need additional weekend shifts, I propose comparing their on-time delivery rate, average delay hours, and order volume over the last eight weeks. I will also separate weekday and weekend performance so we can see whether the problem is concentrated on particular days.

Could you confirm whether carrier performance should be included in the first version? A carrier split may explain part of the delay, but it will add another level of detail. If the staffing decision is the priority, I can first provide the warehouse comparison and add carrier results afterward.

Best,  
Lin

## Baseline Task

在查看任何范例前，用15分钟完成一封120–160词邮件。

虚构公司 BrightCart 本周出现以下变化：

- 结账转化率从4.8%降到4.1%
- 移动端降幅最大，从4.5%降到3.5%
- 网站流量保持稳定
- 周二发布了新的移动支付页面
- 团队尚未证明新版页面就是原因

写给产品经理，说明发生了什么、证据支持什么、还不能确定什么，以及你建议的下一步。

## Baseline Reference

**Subject: Mobile checkout conversion update**

Hi Jordan,

Checkout conversion fell from 4.8% to 4.1% this week while overall traffic remained stable. The decline is concentrated on mobile, where conversion dropped from 4.5% to 3.5%.

The timing is consistent with Tuesday's mobile payment release, but the current data does not prove that the new page caused the change. I recommend checking the payment-step error rate by device and comparing users exposed to the old and new flows. If the error increase is confirmed, we should consider rolling back the mobile page while the product team investigates.

I will share the step-level results by tomorrow noon.

Best,  
Lin

## Oral Retell

不看草稿，用1分钟说清四件事：对方要做什么决策、你建议分析什么、何时交付、还需要确认什么。
