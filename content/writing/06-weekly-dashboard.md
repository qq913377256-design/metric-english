# Writing a Weekly Dashboard Update

## Workplace Context

虚构订阅平台 FieldNote 每周一向业务负责人发送经营看板更新。看板有很多变化，但管理者只需要知道本周最重要的结果、驱动因素和需要决定的事项。

## Data Brief

- 周收入增长6%，达到124万美元
- 新客户收入增长14%
- 老客户扩展收入下降5%
- 企业客户流失3个，其中2个提到实施时间过长
- 支持工单量增长18%，主要来自新导入功能
- 下周要决定是否延长企业客户实施支持

## Model Email

**Subject: Weekly dashboard — growth improved, implementation risk remains**

Hi team,

Weekly revenue reached $1.24 million, up 6%, mainly because new-customer revenue increased 14%. Expansion revenue from existing customers declined 5%, so the overall growth is less broad than the headline suggests.

We also lost three enterprise accounts; two cited a long implementation process. Support tickets rose 18%, mostly for the new import feature.

I recommend reviewing implementation capacity before next week's planning meeting and tracking time-to-first-value for new enterprise accounts.

Best,  
Lin

## Structure Breakdown

1. 标题同时写结果和风险
2. 第一段给主要结果和最重要的驱动因素
3. 第二段只保留需要关注的风险
4. 最后一段给出下一步或需要的决策

## Language Toolkit

- **mainly because...**：说明主要驱动因素
- **less broad than the headline suggests**：给总体增长增加必要语境
- **X cited Y**：说明客户给出的原因
- **I recommend reviewing... before...**：把分析连接到下一次决策

## Model Task

根据以下数据写60–90词周报：

- 周活跃用户增长9%
- 新用户增长15%，老用户增长2%
- 报表导出失败率从1%升至4%
- 下周要决定是否继续推广新导出功能

## Model Reference

**Subject: Weekly product update — user growth with export risk**

Hi team,

Weekly active users increased 9%, led by 15% growth among new users. Existing-user activity rose only 2%, so acquisition explains most of the change.

At the same time, report export failures increased from 1% to 4% after the new feature launch. Before expanding the rollout next week, I recommend reviewing failures by browser and account size and setting a recovery target.

Best,  
Lin

## Guided Task

下面的周报罗列数字，没有优先级：

> Revenue is up 6%. New revenue is up 14%. Expansion is down 5%. Tickets are up 18%. We lost three accounts.

改写成90–130词。先给结论，再解释增长是否健康，指出企业客户风险，并提出一个会议前的动作。

## Guided Reference

**Subject: Weekly dashboard — strong acquisition, weaker account health**

Hi team,

Revenue increased 6% to $1.24 million this week, driven by a 14% rise in new-customer revenue. However, expansion revenue fell 5%, which means the improvement is concentrated in acquisition rather than existing-account growth.

Three enterprise customers also cancelled, and two mentioned a long implementation process. Support tickets increased 18%, mainly for the new import feature.

Before next week's planning meeting, I recommend reviewing implementation workload and the first 30 days of product use for enterprise accounts. This will help us decide whether additional onboarding support should be prioritised.

Best,  
Lin

## Independent Task

虚构物流平台 MoveGrid 本周数据：

- 准时送达率从92%升至94%
- North区域升至97%，South区域从90%降至86%
- South区域订单增长22%，司机在线小时只增长5%
- 投诉量在South增长31%
- 下周要决定是否增加临时司机奖励

写120–160词周报，突出总体结果背后的地区差异，并提出决策建议和需要监控的指标。

## Independent Reference

**Subject: Weekly delivery update — overall gain hides South region risk**

Hi team,

On-time delivery improved from 92% to 94% this week, but the gain was driven by the North region, which reached 97%. South moved in the opposite direction: on-time delivery fell from 90% to 86%, and complaints increased 31%.

South order volume grew 22% while driver online hours rose only 5%. This capacity gap is consistent with the service decline, although route mix and weather should also be checked.

For next week's incentive decision, I recommend testing a temporary driver bonus during South's peak periods rather than applying it across all regions. We should measure accepted orders and on-time delivery while monitoring cost per delivery and cancellation rate.

Best,  
Lin

## Oral Retell

用1分钟讲周报，只保留一个总体结论、两个证据、一个风险和一个下一步。
