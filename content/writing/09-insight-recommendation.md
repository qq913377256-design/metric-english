# Turning an Insight into a Recommendation

## Workplace Context

虚构订阅工具 Formly 的小企业客户在第一次续费时流失较多。你已经找到使用行为差异，需要把洞察写成一个可测试、可衡量、有限风险的建议。

## Data Brief

- 小企业客户首次续费率为68%，其他客户为82%
- 未使用自动化功能的客户续费率为57%
- 使用至少2个自动化流程的客户续费率为79%
- 相关性不能证明自动化使用导致续费
- 客户成功团队可在续费前60天触达约500个账户
- 护栏：客服工单、降级率、顾问工时

## Model Email

**Subject: Pilot guided automation setup before first renewal**

Hi team,

Small-business customers renew at 68%, compared with 82% for other accounts. Within this segment, customers using at least two automation workflows renew at 79%, versus 57% among those using none.

This pattern does not prove that automation causes retention, but it identifies a testable opportunity. I recommend offering guided automation setup to 250 eligible accounts 60 days before renewal and comparing them with 250 similar accounts.

We should measure renewal and workflow adoption while monitoring support tickets, downgrades, and consultant hours.

Best,  
Lin

## Structure Breakdown

1. 用数据明确问题集中在哪个群体
2. 说明支持建议的行为差异
3. 承认证据不能确认因果
4. 给出对象、时间、规模和对照
5. 写清主指标和护栏指标

## Language Toolkit

- **Within this segment...**：继续缩小问题范围
- **does not prove..., but it identifies...**：保留因果边界
- **I recommend offering X to Y before Z**：写清动作、对象和时间
- **while monitoring...**：补充护栏指标

## Model Task

写60–90词建议：

- 新卖家首月留存为54%
- 上传10件以上商品的卖家留存为72%
- 未达到10件的留存为41%
- 建议对300名新卖家测试7天上架引导
- 衡量首月留存，监控客服量

## Model Reference

New sellers retain at 54% after one month. Sellers who upload at least ten products retain at 72%, compared with 41% for those below ten.

This relationship does not confirm causation, but it suggests a useful test. I recommend giving 300 new sellers a seven-day listing guide and comparing them with a similar control group. We should measure product uploads and one-month retention while monitoring support contacts.

## Guided Task

把模糊建议改写成90–130词：

> We should improve small-business retention by teaching customers more features.

使用Data Brief中的数字，写出目标群体、证据边界、试点规模、时点、主指标和至少两个护栏。

## Guided Reference

**Subject: Proposed retention pilot for small-business accounts**

Hi team,

First-renewal rate is 68% for small-business accounts, compared with 82% for other customers. Small-business customers using at least two automation workflows renew at 79%, while those using none renew at 57%.

The difference does not establish that automation use causes renewal, so I recommend a controlled pilot rather than a full programme. Customer Success can offer guided setup to 250 accounts 60 days before renewal and compare them with 250 similar accounts.

The primary measures should be workflow adoption and renewal rate. We should also monitor support tickets, plan downgrades, and consultant hours to understand the cost and possible negative effects.

Best,  
Lin

## Independent Task

虚构外卖平台 MealDash 发现餐厅菜单照片与订单有关：

- 有完整照片的餐厅下单转化率为18%，无完整照片为11%
- 差异在新餐厅最明显
- 图片完整度可能与餐厅运营能力相关，不能确认因果
- 内容团队可为200家新餐厅提供拍摄支持
- 试点成本每家35美元
- 可衡量照片完成率、转化率，护栏为上线时间和退款率

写120–160词建议邮件，包含证据、边界、行动、试点设计、衡量和成本风险。

## Independent Reference

**Subject: Pilot photo support for new restaurants**

Hi team,

Restaurants with complete menu photos convert 18% of menu visits into orders, compared with 11% for restaurants without complete photos. The difference is largest among newly launched restaurants.

Photo coverage may reflect broader operating quality, so the current analysis does not prove that adding photos will create the full conversion difference. I recommend testing photography support with 100 new restaurants and using another 100 eligible restaurants as a comparison group.

At $35 per restaurant, the treatment cost would be $3,500. We should measure photo completion and order conversion over four weeks while monitoring launch time and refund rate. If conversion improves enough to cover the service cost without delaying launch, we can evaluate a wider programme.

Best,  
Lin
