# Explaining a Conversion Drop

## Workplace Context

虚构订阅服务 StreamBee 的试用转付费率连续两周下降。市场负责人想知道是不是新广告带来了低质量流量。你需要给出重点结论，同时避免过早确认原因。

## Data Brief

- 试用转付费率：12.4%降至10.8%
- 付费搜索：13.0%降至8.2%，流量增长45%
- 其他渠道合计：12.1%降至11.9%
- 新广告系列在下降前3天上线
- 目前没有用户层面的广告曝光数据

## Model Email

**Subject: Trial conversion decline is concentrated in paid search**

Hi Marco,

Trial-to-paid conversion fell from 12.4% to 10.8% over the last two weeks. The decline is concentrated in paid search, where conversion dropped from 13.0% to 8.2% while traffic increased by 45%. Other channels were broadly stable.

The timing suggests that the new campaign may have changed the visitor mix, but we cannot confirm this without user-level exposure data. I recommend comparing keyword and landing-page performance first, then adding campaign identifiers to the trial table.

I will share the first breakdown tomorrow.

Best,  
Lin

## Structure Breakdown

1. 给出总体变化和比较时间
2. 指出变化集中在哪个细分群体
3. 把事实和解释分开
4. 提出能够验证解释的下一步

## Language Toolkit

- **fell from X to Y over...**：写清起点、终点和时间
- **The decline is concentrated in...**：指出主要贡献群体
- **were broadly stable**：说明其他部分没有明显变化
- **The timing suggests..., but we cannot confirm...**：提出可能原因并保留边界

## Model Task

写60–90词的趋势说明：

- 退款率从2.1%升至3.0%
- 新客户退款率从2.4%升至4.8%
- 老客户保持1.9%
- 新包装同期上线，但还没有按退款原因拆分

## Model Reference

Refund rate increased from 2.1% to 3.0% this month. The change is concentrated among new customers, whose refund rate rose from 2.4% to 4.8%, while the rate for existing customers remained at 1.9%.

The timing is consistent with the new packaging launch, but we have not yet reviewed the refund reasons. I recommend splitting refunds by reason and delivery date before concluding that packaging caused the increase.

## Guided Task

下面的结论把相关性写成了因果：

> Conversion is down because the new campaign brought bad users. We should stop it immediately.

使用Data Brief中的数字，改写成90–130词。要区分已知事实、可能解释和验证动作，并说明下一次更新时间。

## Guided Reference

**Subject: Update on the trial conversion decline**

Hi Marco,

Trial-to-paid conversion declined from 12.4% to 10.8% in the last two weeks. Paid search explains most of the movement: its conversion rate fell from 13.0% to 8.2% as traffic increased by 45%. Conversion from other channels remained close to its previous level.

The new campaign launched three days before the decline, so a change in traffic quality is a plausible explanation. However, we do not yet have user-level exposure data and should not treat the relationship as confirmed.

I will review keywords and landing pages today and share an updated assessment by tomorrow afternoon.

Best,  
Lin

## Independent Reference

**Subject: First-order completion decline on Android**

Hi Sara,

First-order completion fell from 68% to 63% after last week's release. The decline is isolated to Android, where completion dropped from 67% to 56%. iOS remained at 69%, and the Android traffic volume and regional mix were stable.

Address validation errors increased during the same period, which makes the new address selector a likely area to investigate. We have not completed the root-cause review, so the current evidence does not yet prove that the release caused the full decline.

I recommend comparing each address step before and after the release and checking the affected app versions. I will share the error breakdown by noon tomorrow. If one version accounts for most failures, the product team can then assess a rollback.

Best,  
Lin
