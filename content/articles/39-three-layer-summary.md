## English Original

### Write an Executive Summary in Three Layers

Fictional retailer OakThread sent leaders a twelve-page analysis of falling repeat purchases. The answer existed on page nine, after methodology and charts. Executives asked for a meeting because they could not identify the conclusion or requested decision quickly.

The analyst rewrote the summary in three layers. Layer one was a two-sentence decision headline: repeat purchase fell mainly among new mobile customers after delivery estimates became less accurate; restore conservative estimates in two regions while testing a new calculation. Layer two supplied three evidence bullets with size, comparison, and affected segment. Layer three explained uncertainty, alternatives, financial impact, owner, and next checkpoint.

Details remained available through links and an appendix. The summary did not remove complexity; it ordered information by decision value. The first layer could stand alone in an inbox. The second let a leader test whether evidence supported the claim. The third showed trade-offs and execution. Methods followed for readers who needed audit depth.

The analyst used calibrated language. Delivery estimates “explained most of the observed decline” because a natural comparison supported the relationship, but the evidence was not a randomized experiment. She quantified the unexplained portion and named a competing seasonal explanation. The action was reversible and limited, matching the uncertainty.

After the change, leaders approved the regional test asynchronously. A strong executive summary is not a compressed data dump. It is a layered path from conclusion to evidence to decision. Readers with different time and expertise can stop at the appropriate depth without losing the main message.

The summary also preserved a link to reproducible queries and metric definitions. Concision did not excuse weak evidence. Instead, it made the claim easy to inspect: executives saw the decision first, while reviewers could follow each number back to its source and method.

## Chinese Translation
虚构零售商OakThread向管理层发送了一份十二页的复购下降分析。真正答案位于第九页，前面都是方法和图表。管理层无法快速找到结论和需要决定的事项，因此又要求召开会议。

分析师把摘要改写为三层。第一层是两句决策标题：配送时间预估变得不准确后，新移动端客户的复购下降最明显；建议在两个区域恢复更保守的预估，同时测试新的计算方法。第二层用三条证据说明变化规模、比较基准和受影响人群。第三层说明不确定性、替代解释、财务影响、负责人和下一检查点。

详细内容仍然通过链接和附录提供。摘要没有删除复杂信息，而是按照决策价值重新排序。第一层可以独立出现在邮件正文；第二层让管理层判断证据是否支持结论；第三层展示取舍与执行方式；需要审计深度的读者还可以继续查看方法。

分析师使用与证据强度匹配的措辞。她写“配送预估解释了观察到的大部分下降”，因为自然对照支持这种关系，但证据并非随机实验。她量化尚未解释的部分，并提出季节性活动这一替代解释。建议行动可以撤销且范围有限，与当前不确定性相匹配。

修改后，管理层无需开会就批准了区域测试。一份好的管理层摘要不是压缩后的数据堆积，而是一条从结论到证据再到决策的分层路径。拥有不同时间和专业背景的读者都可以在合适的深度停下，同时不会错过核心信息。

摘要还保留了可复现查询和指标定义的链接。简洁不能成为证据薄弱的借口，它应该让结论更容易检查：管理层先看到决策，复核人员则可以把每个数字追溯到来源和方法。
## Vocabulary
- **decision headline** — 决策标题；a conclusion and requested action.
- **calibrated language** — 校准措辞；language matching evidence strength.
- **asynchronously** — 异步地；without a live meeting.
- **audit depth** — 审计深度；detail needed to verify work.
- **appendix** — 附录；supporting detail placed after the main message.
- **alternative explanation** — 替代解释；another plausible reason for the result.
- **reproducible query** — 可复现查询；code another analyst can rerun and verify.
- **checkpoint** — 检查点；a planned time to review progress and evidence.
## Grammar Analysis
- “who needed audit depth”用who限定读者。
- “matching the uncertainty”现在分词短语说明行动为何合适。
## Data Analyst Extension
摘要模板：2-sentence conclusion/request；3 evidence bullets；uncertainty/impact/owner/checkpoint；detail links。
## Reading Questions
1. What were the three layers?
2. Why was causal language limited?
3. How did action match uncertainty?
### Workplace Application
Turn a long analysis into a 40–80 word executive summary with conclusion, two facts, uncertainty, and a decision request.
### Answer Key
1. The layers were decision headline, core evidence, and uncertainty plus execution detail.
2. The evidence came from a natural comparison rather than a randomized experiment.
3. The recommended action was limited and reversible.

### Application Model Response

Repeat purchase fell mainly among new mobile customers after delivery estimates became less accurate. Their repeat rate declined eight points, and two regions explain 71% of the gap. The relationship is strong but not experimental. I request approval to restore conservative estimates in those regions for four weeks while we test a new calculation.
