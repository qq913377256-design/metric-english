## English Original

### Explain a Variance Without Guessing

Fictional software provider WillowCRM missed its monthly revenue target by 8%. A hurried update said the miss was caused by weak demand. Sales objected because qualified opportunities had actually increased. The analyst had turned an untested explanation into a fact, damaging trust before the real drivers were known.

She restarted with a variance bridge. Revenue was $480,000 below target. Delayed enterprise renewals explained $260,000, lower average deal size explained $140,000, and favorable new-customer volume added $70,000. The remaining $150,000 was not yet allocated because discounts, timing, and foreign exchange overlapped. Contributions were labeled measured, estimated, or unknown.

The update separated four layers. First came the observed fact: revenue and target. Second came quantified contributors supported by records. Third came hypotheses requiring more evidence. Fourth came next actions, owners, and dates. The analyst wrote, “Renewal timing is the largest confirmed contributor. We are testing whether approval delays or customer decisions explain the shift.” This was more precise than “enterprise customers are delaying because budgets are weak.”

The team checked whether the target and actuals used the same currency rates, booking rules, and month boundaries. It compared the shortfall by product, region, customer size, and sales stage, but avoided double-counting overlapping drivers. A waterfall chart supported the bridge; a short note explained limitations.

Leaders did not need certainty before acting. They approved follow-up on the ten largest renewals and a discount review while keeping the demand hypothesis open. Good variance communication does not hide what is unknown. It prevents uncertainty from being confused with ignorance by showing what has been verified, how much it explains, and what will reduce the remaining gap. Analysts earn credibility when their confidence is proportional to their evidence.

At the next review, the analyst updated each hypothesis rather than rewriting history. One was confirmed, one was rejected, and the unexplained balance shrank. This visible learning process helped stakeholders understand why conclusions could change as evidence accumulated.

## Chinese Translation

WillowCRM收入比目标低8%，仓促归因于需求疲软，但合格机会反而增加。分析师改用差异桥：续约延迟、客单价和新增客户分别量化贡献，剩余部分标为未知，并区分已测量、估算和未知。

更新按事实、已证实贡献、待验证假设、行动与负责人四层表达，同时核对币种、确认规则和月度边界。管理层可以先处理最大续约，却不必把未验证需求假设写成事实。

## Vocabulary

- **variance bridge** — 差异桥；a quantified breakdown of a gap.
- **contributor** — 贡献因素；a factor explaining part of a change.
- **allocate** — 归因分配；assign a portion of variance to a driver.
- **overlap** — 重叠；drivers explaining some of the same value.
- **proportional** — 成比例的；matched in degree or strength.

## Grammar Analysis

- “whether approval delays or customer decisions explain the shift”是whether...or...宾语从句。
- “what **has been verified**”使用现在完成时被动语态。

## Data Analyst Extension

差异说明固定输出actual、target、gap、confirmed drivers、hypotheses、unexplained、actions。贡献和应接近总差异，重叠项要明确处理。

## Reading Questions

1. Why was the first explanation weak?
2. What were the four layers?
3. How did leaders act under uncertainty?

### Workplace Application

Costs are 12% above plan; freight explains five points, while supplier pricing is only suspected. Write a 40–80 word variance update with confidence labels and next steps.

### Answer Key

1. It presented an untested cause as a fact.
2. The four layers were fact, confirmed drivers, hypotheses, and action.
3. Leaders acted on confirmed renewals while keeping the demand hypothesis open.

### Application Model Response

Costs are 12% above plan. Higher freight expense is a confirmed contributor and explains five percentage points. Supplier pricing may explain part of the remaining gap, but that cause is not yet verified. Procurement will reconcile invoice prices by supplier by Thursday, while Operations reviews shipment mix and identifies immediate freight actions.
