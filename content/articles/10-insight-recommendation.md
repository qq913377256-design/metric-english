# From Insight to Recommendation

## English Original

An analysis is rarely finished when a pattern has been found. Decision-makers still need to understand why the pattern matters, what action the evidence supports, and what could go wrong. Turning an insight into a recommendation requires more than adding “therefore” to the final slide.

Consider a subscription company where annual-plan cancellations have increased by 18%. The increase is concentrated among small business accounts during their first renewal. Interviews and support records show that many of these customers did not use the advanced features included in the higher-priced plan.

A weak recommendation would be “improve retention.” It names the desired outcome but gives no practical choice. A stronger recommendation is more specific: test a guided feature setup for new small business accounts 60 days before renewal, starting with customers who have used fewer than two advanced features.

The recommendation connects four elements. The evidence identifies an at-risk group. The proposed action addresses a plausible reason for cancellation. A pilot limits cost and allows the company to measure impact. Guardrails, such as support contacts and plan downgrades, reveal possible negative effects.

The analyst should also state uncertainty. The evidence is consistent with low feature adoption causing cancellations, but observational data cannot establish the effect as confidently as a randomised test. Other factors, including price sensitivity, may contribute.

Good recommendations make a decision easier without pretending that uncertainty has disappeared. They connect evidence, expected impact, action, measurement, and risk in language that a responsible owner can use.

## Chinese Translation

发现一个模式时，分析工作通常还没有结束。决策者仍然需要理解这个模式为什么重要、证据支持什么行动，以及可能出现什么问题。把洞察转化为建议，不能只在最后一页加一个因此

以一家订阅公司为例，年付计划取消量上升18%。增幅集中在第一次续费的小企业账户。访谈和客服记录显示，许多这类客户没有使用高价计划中包含的高级功能

改进留存是一条很弱的建议。它说出了想要的结果，却没有提供可执行选择。更具体的建议是：在续费前60天，为新的小企业账户测试引导式功能设置，优先覆盖使用高级功能少于两个的客户

这条建议连接了四个要素。证据识别出高风险群体；拟议行动针对一个合理的取消原因；小范围试点控制成本，并让公司能够衡量影响；客服咨询量和套餐降级等护栏指标可以揭示潜在负面影响

分析师还应该说明不确定性。现有证据与低功能使用导致取消这一解释一致，但观察性数据无法像随机实验那样有力地确认效果。价格敏感等其他因素也可能产生影响

好的建议让决策更容易，同时承认不确定性仍然存在。它用负责人可以采用的语言，把证据、预期影响、行动、测量和风险连接起来

## Vocabulary

- **decision-maker**：决策者
- **recommendation** /ˌrekəmenˈdeɪʃən/：基于证据提出的行动建议
- **be concentrated among**：集中出现在某个群体中
- **renewal** /rɪˈnuːəl/：续费、续约
- **at-risk group**：高风险群体
- **plausible** /ˈplɔːzəbəl/：合理可信的
- **pilot** /ˈpaɪlət/：在全面推广前进行的小规模试点
- **observational data**：在非随机实验环境中观察得到的数据

## Grammar Analysis

### 1. where引导定语从句

`Consider a subscription company where annual-plan cancellations have increased by 18%.`

`where`在这里表示在这家公司中，引出案例背景。`have increased`强调变化延续到当前分析时点

### 2. who引导定语从句

`Start with customers who have used fewer than two advanced features.`

`who have used...`限定试点优先覆盖的客户。`fewer than`修饰可数名词`features`

### 3. be consistent with

`The evidence is consistent with low feature adoption causing cancellations.`

`be consistent with`表示证据和某个解释相符，但没有声称已经证明因果。它比`proves that`更谨慎

### 4. without加动名词

`Good recommendations make a decision easier without pretending that uncertainty has disappeared.`

`without pretending`说明在不做某事的前提下实现主句目标，适合补充方法边界

## Data Analyst Extension

建议可以使用五部分结构：

1. **Evidence**：什么变化集中在哪个群体，规模多大
2. **Interpretation**：最可信的解释是什么，还有哪些替代解释
3. **Action**：谁在什么时间对什么对象采取什么行动
4. **Measurement**：用什么主要指标和护栏指标判断效果
5. **Risk**：成本、适用范围和不确定性是什么

表达模板：`Because cancellations are concentrated among first-renewal small business accounts with low feature adoption, we recommend testing guided setup 60 days before renewal. We will measure renewal rate and monitor support contacts and downgrades.`

## Reading Questions

1. Why is “improve retention” a weak recommendation?
2. Which customers should enter the proposed pilot first?
3. How do guardrail metrics improve the recommendation?
4. Why does the analyst avoid claiming that low feature adoption definitely causes cancellation?
5. Write one recommendation using evidence from your own work

### Answer Key

1. It names an outcome but does not specify a practical action, owner, or target group
2. New small business accounts that have used fewer than two advanced features
3. They reveal possible negative effects while the pilot measures its main outcome
4. The evidence is observational, and other factors such as price sensitivity may contribute
5. Sample answer: Because late deliveries are concentrated in two warehouses, test an earlier daily cut-off there and measure on-time delivery and cancellation rate
