# Cohorts Tell a Clearer Retention Story

## English Original

A language-learning app reports that 28% of its users return four weeks after signing up. The number has stayed almost unchanged for six months, so the product team believes retention is stable. Elena, an analyst, is not convinced that the overall average tells the full story.

She groups users by the week in which they joined. Each group is a cohort. The cohort view reveals a pattern hidden by the average. Retention for users who joined before a major product update was falling. Retention for users who joined after the update was improving. Because the company was also spending more on acquisition, the growing number of new users made the overall rate look flat.

Elena then separates users by acquisition channel. Organic users have the highest retention, while users from one paid campaign leave quickly. The product update appears helpful, but the campaign is bringing in people who are less interested in long-term learning.

The team changes two plans. It keeps the new product experience and reduces spending on the weak campaign. It also agrees to review retention by cohort rather than relying only on a blended average.

Cohort analysis does not automatically explain why behaviour changes. It does, however, show when a change begins and which groups move differently. That is often the first step toward a useful explanation.

## Chinese Translation

一款语言学习应用报告称，28%的用户在注册四周后仍会回来。这个数字连续六个月几乎没有变化，因此产品团队认为留存表现稳定。分析师Elena认为总体平均值没有讲出完整情况

她按照用户加入的周次分组，每一组就是一个同期群。同期群视图揭示了被平均值隐藏的模式。重大产品更新前加入的用户，留存率正在下降；更新后加入的用户，留存率正在改善。由于公司同时增加了获客投入，不断增长的新用户数量让总体留存率看起来保持不变

Elena随后按获客渠道拆分用户。自然流量用户的留存率最高，而来自某个付费活动的用户很快离开。产品更新似乎有效，但这个活动带来的用户对长期学习兴趣较低

团队调整了两项计划。他们保留新的产品体验，减少表现较弱活动的预算，还约定以后按同期群检查留存，不再只依赖混合平均值

同期群分析不会自动解释行为为什么改变，但它能显示变化从何时开始，以及哪些群体出现不同走势。这往往是找到有效解释的第一步

## Vocabulary

- **retention** /rɪˈtenʃən/：留存，用户在一段时间后继续使用产品
- **cohort** /ˈkoʊhɔːrt/：具有共同起始时间或特征的一组对象
- **overall average**：总体平均值
- **reveal** /rɪˈviːl/：揭示原本不明显的信息
- **acquisition** /ˌækwɪˈzɪʃən/：用户获取、获客
- **organic user**：通过自然渠道进入的用户
- **blended** /ˈblendɪd/：混合计算的
- **rely on**：依赖

## Grammar Analysis

### 1. 介词加关系代词

`She groups users by the week in which they joined.`

`in which`表示用户在哪一周加入，也可以写成更口语的`the week that they joined`

### 2. 过去进行时

`The company was also spending more on acquisition.`

`was spending`强调在观察留存变化的同一时期，获客投入也在持续增加

### 3. do表示强调

`It does, however, show when a change begins.`

前一句说同期群分析不能自动解释原因，这里用`does show`强调它确实能提供的价值。`however`插入句中表示转折

## Data Analyst Extension

留存同期群表通常包含：

- 行：注册周或首次购买月
- 列：从起始事件算起的第0周、第1周、第2周
- 单元格：仍活跃的用户占该同期群初始人数的比例

汇报时避免只说留存上升。明确群体和窗口：`Week-4 retention for users acquired after the May onboarding update rose from 24% to 31%.`

同期群可以控制起始时间差异，但不能自动排除渠道、地区或用户意图等其他因素

## Reading Questions

1. Why does the overall retention rate look stable?
2. What does the cohort view reveal about the product update?
3. Which users have the highest retention?
4. What can cohort analysis show, and what can it not explain by itself?

### Answer Key

1. A growing number of newly acquired users hides different trends among older and newer cohorts
2. Retention improved for users who joined after the update
3. Organic users have the highest retention
4. It can show when change begins and which groups differ, but it cannot prove the reason by itself
