# Building a Useful Weekly Dashboard

## English Original

Every Friday, the growth team receives a dashboard with 42 charts. It takes several minutes to load and much longer to understand. Most people scroll through it, look for a green number, and close the page. The dashboard contains a great deal of data, yet it supports very few decisions.

Priya, the new analyst, begins by interviewing the people who use the report. The marketing lead needs to decide where to move campaign budget. The product manager wants to see whether new users complete onboarding. The country managers need to spot unusual regional changes.

Priya redesigns the dashboard around these decisions. The first page contains six core metrics with a four-week comparison. Each metric has a short definition. The second page breaks results down by channel and country. Instead of showing every possible chart, it highlights changes that are larger than a practical threshold.

She also adds a short written summary: what changed, the most likely reason, and what the team should investigate next. After one month, the meeting becomes shorter. Managers spend less time asking what a chart means and more time discussing action.

A useful dashboard is not a storage room for charts. It is a shared view of the facts that matter for the next decision.

## Grammar Analysis

### 1. whether引导宾语从句

`The product manager wants to see whether new users complete onboarding.`

`whether`表示是否，引出需要确认的问题。它比直接使用一般疑问句更适合嵌入长句

### 2. instead of加动名词

`Instead of showing every possible chart, it highlights important changes.`

`instead of`后接名词或动名词，表示没有采用前一种做法，而采用后一种做法

### 3. 比较结构

`Managers spend less time asking what a chart means and more time discussing action.`

`spend less time doing A and more time doing B`适合描述流程优化带来的时间分配变化

## Data Analyst Extension

周报仪表盘可以按以下层级组织：

- **Headline**：本周最重要的变化和行动
- **Core metrics**：少量稳定指标及历史比较
- **Diagnostics**：按渠道、地区、产品等维度拆分
- **Definitions**：口径、更新时间和数据负责人

摘要模板：`Activation fell 3.2 percentage points this week, mainly among mobile users from paid search. We are checking the new sign-up flow before changing campaign spend.`

## Reading Questions

1. Why is the original dashboard difficult to use?
2. What decisions do the three groups need to make?
3. What does Priya put on the first page?
4. How does the redesigned dashboard change the weekly meeting?

### Answer Key

1. It has too many charts, loads slowly, and is not organised around decisions
2. They decide on campaign budget, monitor onboarding, and investigate regional changes
3. Six core metrics, a four-week comparison, and short metric definitions
4. Meetings become shorter and managers spend more time discussing action
