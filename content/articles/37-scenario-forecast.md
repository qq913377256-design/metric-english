## English Original

### Forecast with Scenarios, Not One Number

Fictional hardware company LakeForge forecast next-quarter demand at exactly 120,000 units. Procurement treated it as a promise and ordered components accordingly. When a channel partner delayed promotion, demand reached 96,000 and excess inventory grew. The forecast had hidden its assumptions and uncertainty.

The analyst replaced the point estimate with three scenarios. The base case assumed normal renewal and the planned promotion, producing 112,000 units. The upside case assumed stronger conversion and no supply interruption, producing 128,000. The downside case assumed promotion delay and weaker repeat orders, producing 92,000. Each scenario linked numbers to observable conditions.

The team identified trigger indicators: partner campaign date, weekly conversion, cancellation rate, and component lead time. Procurement committed to the downside volume first and reserved flexible capacity for additional units. Marketing had actions for each trigger. The forecast was reviewed weekly, but prior versions remained visible so accuracy and bias could be evaluated.

Scenarios are not three arbitrary guesses. They are coherent combinations of assumptions that help leaders prepare for distinct futures. Ranges should not become so wide that they avoid responsibility. Analysts should state which outcome is most likely, what evidence would shift that view, and which decisions are robust across cases.

LakeForge reduced excess inventory while preserving upside capacity. The conversation changed from “Is 120,000 correct?” to “What will we do if promotion or conversion changes?” A forecast creates value when it supports adaptable decisions, not when it offers false certainty.

Finance also compared actual results with every prior scenario. That record showed whether estimates were consistently optimistic and whether trigger indicators arrived early enough to guide action. Forecast review became a learning system rather than a search for one person to blame.

Leaders could therefore commit resources in stages, preserving options instead of making one irreversible purchase against a fragile estimate.
This flexibility had measurable value.

## Chinese Translation
虚构硬件公司LakeForge预测下一季度需求正好是12万件。采购团队把这个数字当作承诺，并据此订购零部件。当渠道合作伙伴推迟促销后，实际需求只有9.6万件，库存大量积压。原来的预测隐藏了假设和不确定性。

分析师用三个情景替代单点数字。基准情景假设续购正常且促销按计划执行，预计11.2万件；上行情景假设转化更强且供应不中断，预计12.8万件；下行情景假设促销推迟且复购下降，预计9.2万件。每个数字都与可以观察的业务条件相连。

团队确定了四个触发指标：合作伙伴促销日期、每周转化率、取消率和零部件提前期。采购先承诺下行情景所需的数量，再为额外产量预留弹性产能。市场团队也为每种触发情况准备相应动作。预测每周更新，但以前的版本会被保留，用于评估准确性和系统性偏差。

情景不是三个随意猜出的数字，而是帮助管理层为不同未来做好准备的一组连贯假设。预测范围也不能宽到完全逃避责任。分析师需要说明哪个结果最可能发生、什么证据会改变判断，以及哪些决策在多个情景下都保持稳健。

LakeForge在保留上行产能的同时减少了过剩库存。讨论从“12万是否正确”转变为“如果促销或转化发生变化，我们要采取什么动作”。预测只有在支持可调整的决策时才创造价值，而不是提供虚假确定性。

财务团队还会把实际结果与所有历史情景比较，检查预测是否持续过于乐观，以及触发指标是否足够早地出现。预测复盘因此成为学习系统，而不是寻找责任人的过程。管理层也能够分阶段投入资源、保留选择权，避免根据脆弱的单点估计做出一次性且不可逆的采购。
## Vocabulary
- **point estimate** — 点估计；one forecast number.
- **trigger indicator** — 触发指标；a signal causing an action or update.
- **lead time** — 提前期；time needed before delivery.
- **robust** — 稳健的；effective across several scenarios.
- **base case** — 基准情景；the most likely forecast under current assumptions.
- **upside case** — 上行情景；a forecast with favorable conditions.
- **flexible capacity** — 弹性产能；capacity that can be added when signals improve.
- **forecast bias** — 预测偏差；a repeated tendency to forecast too high or too low.

## Reading Questions
1. What did the point forecast hide?
2. How did procurement respond?
3. What makes scenarios coherent?
### Workplace Application
Demand depends on a partner launch date. Write a 40–80 word three-scenario forecast note with one trigger and action.
### Answer Key
1. The point forecast hid assumptions and uncertainty.
2. Procurement committed to downside volume and reserved flexible capacity.
3. Coherent scenarios connect a consistent set of assumptions to observable conditions.

### Application Model Response

The base case is 112,000 units if the partner launches on June 3. The upside case is 128,000 with stronger conversion; the downside is 92,000 if launch slips two weeks. We should commit to downside volume now and reserve flexible capacity. A confirmed June 3 launch will trigger the next component order.
