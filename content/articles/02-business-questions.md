# Start with a Business Question

## English Original

One morning, a manager sends Leo a short message: “Please analyse our customers.” Leo could open the database at once, but the request is too wide. There are many possible customer questions. The manager may care about new customers, repeat buyers, unhappy users, or high-value accounts.

Leo asks three questions before he starts. What decision do you need to make? Which customer group are you worried about? What time period should we study? The manager explains that the marketing team must choose a campaign for next month. They want to know which first-time buyers are likely to return.

The new question is much clearer: “Which first-time buyers made a second purchase within 30 days, and what did they buy first?” Now Leo can define the customer group, the time window, and the outcome. He can also explain what the analysis cannot prove.

A clear business question saves time for both the analyst and the manager. It connects the data work to a real decision. Good analysis often begins before anyone writes a query.

新的问题清楚多了：哪些首次购买者在30天内完成了第二次购买，他们第一次买了什么？现在Leo可以定义客户群、时间窗口和结果，也可以说明这项分析不能证明什么

清楚的业务问题能节省分析师和经理双方的时间。它把数据工作和真实决策连接起来。好的分析往往在写查询之前就已经开始

## Vocabulary

- **request** /rɪˈkwest/：请求、需求
- **possible** /ˈpɑːsəbəl/：可能的
- **repeat buyer**：复购客户
- **account** /əˈkaʊnt/：客户账户，B2B语境中也可指客户公司
- **campaign** /kæmˈpeɪn/：营销活动
- **be likely to**：很可能做某事
- **time window**：分析采用的时间窗口
- **outcome** /ˈaʊtkʌm/：结果、需要衡量的目标变量

## Grammar Analysis

### 1. 间接问题

`They want to know which first-time buyers are likely to return.`

`which first-time buyers`引出想知道的对象。间接问题使用陈述语序，所以是`buyers are`，不是`are buyers`

### 2. before引导时间关系

`Leo asks three questions before he starts.`

`before`说明动作顺序。分析工作中常用`Before we compare the groups, we need to check the sample size.`

### 3. 情态动词cannot

`He can also explain what the analysis cannot prove.`

`cannot prove`明确分析边界。相关表达还有`does not show`、`cannot confirm`和`should not be interpreted as`

## Data Analyst Extension

收到模糊需求时，可以用四个维度澄清：

- **Decision**：`What decision will this analysis support?`
- **Population**：`Which users or products are in scope?`
- **Period**：`What time period should we use?`
- **Measure**：`How will we define success?`

把`Analyse user engagement`改写为：`Did weekly active usage among new team accounts improve within 28 days after onboarding changed?`

## Reading Questions

1. Why does Leo not start with the database?
2. What decision does the marketing team need to make?
3. What three parts can Leo define after the request becomes clear?
4. Rewrite this request: “Please analyse sales.”

### Answer Key

1. The request is too wide and can mean many different things
2. They need to choose a campaign for next month
3. He can define the customer group, time window, and outcome
4. Sample answer: Which product categories had lower weekly sales than their three-month average in July?
