# A Forecast Is a Range, Not a Promise

## English Original

At the start of the quarter, a finance director asks for next month's revenue forecast. The planning team would like one number for the budget file. Daniel, the analyst, provides a central estimate of 4.8 million yuan, but he also presents a likely range from 4.3 to 5.4 million.

The range is not a sign that the model failed. Revenue depends on factors that cannot be known exactly in advance: customer demand, campaign performance, delivery capacity, and the date of a planned product launch. Historical data can describe how these factors behaved before, but it cannot remove future uncertainty.

Daniel builds three scenarios. The base case assumes that recent conversion and traffic trends continue. The downside case includes a two-week launch delay and weaker paid traffic. The upside case assumes an on-time launch and stronger repeat purchases. For each scenario, he records the assumptions that have the greatest effect on revenue.

When actual results arrive, the team compares them with both the forecast and its assumptions. Revenue reaches 4.5 million, which is below the central estimate but inside the expected range. The main difference comes from the launch delay, not from a sudden fall in underlying demand.

A forecast is useful when it helps people prepare for several plausible futures. Presenting a single number without its uncertainty may look precise, but it can lead to a brittle plan.

## Chinese Translation

季度开始时，财务总监要求提供下个月的收入预测。规划团队希望预算文件里只有一个数字。分析师Daniel给出480万元的中心估计，同时也提供430万至540万元的可能区间

这个区间不代表模型失败。收入取决于一些无法提前准确得知的因素：客户需求、营销活动表现、配送能力和计划中产品发布的日期。历史数据可以描述这些因素过去如何变化，但无法消除未来的不确定性

Daniel建立三个情景。基准情景假设近期转化率和流量趋势延续；下行情景包含产品延迟两周发布和付费流量减弱；上行情景假设产品按时发布，复购表现更强。对于每个情景，他都记录对收入影响最大的假设

实际结果出来后，团队把结果同时与预测值和预测假设比较。收入达到450万元，低于中心估计，但仍在预期区间内。主要差异来自产品发布延迟，而不是底层需求突然下降

当预测能帮助人们为几种合理的未来做好准备时，它才有价值。只给出一个没有不确定性的数字，看起来可能很精确，却容易产生缺乏韧性的计划

## Vocabulary

- **central estimate**：中心估计，预测分布中最具代表性的数值
- **likely range**：大概率可能出现的范围
- **in advance**：提前
- **scenario** /səˈnærioʊ/：情景、场景假设
- **base case**：基准情景
- **downside** /ˈdaʊnsaɪd/：下行情景或不利情况
- **plausible** /ˈplɔːzəbəl/：合理可信、可能发生的
- **brittle** /ˈbrɪtəl/：脆弱的，条件稍变就失效的

## Grammar Analysis

### 1. 同位语冒号

`Revenue depends on factors that cannot be known exactly in advance: customer demand, campaign performance, delivery capacity, and the launch date.`

冒号后的列表具体说明`factors`。`that cannot be known`是定语从句，限定这些因素

### 2. which引导非限定性定语从句

`Revenue reaches 4.5 million, which is below the central estimate but inside the expected range.`

逗号后的`which`补充评价前面的整个数值结果，不用于限定是哪一个收入

### 3. when引导条件

`A forecast is useful when it helps people prepare for several plausible futures.`

这里的`when`接近在某种条件下，说明预测有用的判断标准

## Data Analyst Extension

预测沟通应同时呈现：

- 点估计或中心估计
- 基于历史误差或模型分布得到的区间
- 对结果影响最大的假设
- 上行和下行情景对应的行动
- 实际结果出来后的误差复盘

谨慎表达：`Under the base-case assumptions, revenue is expected to be between 4.3 and 5.4 million yuan.`

避免写`Revenue will be 4.8 million`，因为`will`容易让估计听起来像承诺

## Reading Questions

1. Why does Daniel give a range as well as a central estimate?
2. How do the three scenarios differ?
3. Is revenue of 4.5 million a failed forecast? Why?
4. Which assumption would matter most in a forecast from your work?

### Answer Key

1. Important future factors are uncertain, so one number would hide useful information
2. They use different assumptions about the launch, paid traffic, and repeat purchases
3. No. It is below the centre but inside the expected range, and the difference can be linked to a delayed launch
4. Sample answer: Customer renewal rate would have the greatest effect on a subscription forecast
